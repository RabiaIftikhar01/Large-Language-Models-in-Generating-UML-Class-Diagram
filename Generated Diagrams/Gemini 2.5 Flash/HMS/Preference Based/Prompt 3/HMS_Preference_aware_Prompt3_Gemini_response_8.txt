' Patient uses a BillingPolicy (Strategy pattern)
Patient "1" *-- "1" BillingPolicy : <<uses>> currentPolicy
note on link: Patient delegates cost calculation to its assigned BillingPolicy.
This allows dynamic interchangeability of billing rules.

' Invoice is generated for a Patient
Patient "1" --> "0..*" Invoice : generates / billedTo
note on link: An Invoice is generated for a specific Patient.

' Patient aggregates treatments received during their stay
Patient "1" o-- "0..*" Treatment : received / performed
note on link: Patient records all treatments received, which form the basis of billing.

' Invoice contains multiple InvoiceLineItems
Invoice "1" o-- "0..*" InvoiceLineItem : includes
note on link: An Invoice aggregates line items, each representing a treatment with its final adjusted cost.

' InvoiceLineItem refers to a specific Treatment
InvoiceLineItem "1" --> "1" Treatment : refersTo
note on link: Each line item references the original treatment details.

' BillingPolicy needs to access Patient and Treatment details to calculate cost
BillingPolicy ..> Patient : <<accesses>>
BillingPolicy ..> Treatment : <<accesses>>
note on link: The billing policy requires patient and treatment context to apply specific rules.
