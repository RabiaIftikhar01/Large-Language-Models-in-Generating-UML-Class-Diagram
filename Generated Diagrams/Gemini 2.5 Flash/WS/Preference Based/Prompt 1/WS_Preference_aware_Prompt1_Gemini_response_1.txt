note "Represents a physical weather station deployed in the field, capable of hosting multiple sensors and transmitting data." as N_WS
note "An individual sensing device within a weather station, reporting specific environmental measurements." as N_Sensor
note "Encapsulates a single measurement reading from a sensor, storing both its original raw format and its standardized, normalized value." as N_SensorData

note on link WeatherStation::transmissionFrequencyHours
  Defines how often (in hours) a station
  is configured to send data. This can vary.
end note

note on link SensorData::rawValue
  The measurement as received directly
  from the sensor, in its original format.
end note

note on link SensorData::normalizedValue
  The measurement after being converted
  to a standard, consistent format
  for platform-wide use.
end note
